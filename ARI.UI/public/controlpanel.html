Persistent Threads
Keep conversations after they expire
Off by default: when a conversation expires it is gone for good and only its memories remain. Turn this on and expired conversations — everyone's, guests included — are saved in full on this server, shown greyed in the sidebar, and picked up again when someone sends a message in one. Admins can see all of them.
Memory and other people
Let Engram remember conversations with other people
Off by default: Engram only remembers your own conversations. Turn this on and it also remembers server channels, direct messages with other people and guests' chats. Anything someone says there can become a note, so a guest's words end up in the brain, and notes from a conversation with several people can attribute things to the wrong person. What ARI recalls for them is still filtered. Applies immediately.
Dreaming
Let Ari dream when idle
When enabled, Ari enters a dream state after two minutes of inactivity — she explores her memory and projects freely using read-only tools. No user is present; she self-drives until she wakes or a message arrives.
Logging
What ARI records for debugging and for the Deep Thread Inspection page: every request the model sees, its reasoning and its tool calls. Changes are saved straight away but take effect when ARI next starts.
Configurations

Save a full snapshot of your A·R·I setup — servers, models, agents, persona, voice settings, calendar, coding conventions and more. Restoring a config stops all running servers, applies the snapshot, then boots any server with Auto-start on. Credentials and voice training data are never included — configs are safe to share.

Engram sweep interval

How many message exchanges an active conversation accumulates before Engram sweeps it into memory mid-conversation, instead of waiting for the thread to go idle or be closed. Set to 0 to disable interval sweeping (Engram will still sweep on close/dormant as usual). Applies immediately — no restart needed.
